## What changed
The orders service implements `POST /auth/login`, `POST /auth/logout` and
`GET /auth/me`, closes every staff operation with the session guard, and
refuses state-changing staff requests that are not same-origin JSON (403).
It reads two new environment variables.

## What the others relied on (before → after)
- Environment (devops): none → `PUBLIC_ORIGIN` (origin of the web app,
  `scheme://host[:port]`; default `http://localhost:5173` outside production,
  **required** when `NODE_ENV=production`) and `SESSION_SECRET` (at least 32
  characters; **required** in production; outside production a random key per
  process is used when unset). The orders image sets `NODE_ENV=production`, so
  `docker compose up --build` fails at startup until compose sets both
  (spec staff-auth, task 3).
- Contract (frontend): `POST /auth/logout` and `POST /orders/{number}/status`
  → also declare `403` (`components/responses/Forbidden`, problem+json).
  Additive. Regenerate `schema.d.ts`.
- Behaviour (frontend): every staff operation → 401 without a valid session
  (also the ones that still answer 501). State-changing staff requests must
  send `Origin` equal to `PUBLIC_ORIGIN` (browsers do on same-origin `fetch`
  POSTs) and, when they have a body, `Content-Type: application/json`;
  logout may be sent without a body. The session cookie is `session`
  (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Path=/, Secure in production, no Max-Age).
- Responses (frontend, devops): every response carries `Cache-Control:
  no-store`, a CSP `default-src 'none'`, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`,
  `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, and HSTS in production.
